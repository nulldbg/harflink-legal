# Delete Your HarfLink Account & Data

_Last updated: December 27, 2025_

If you no longer want to use **HarfLink**, you can request deletion of your account and associated data.

---

## How to request account deletion

To delete your HarfLink account, please contact us using **one** of the following methods:

1. **By email**

   Send an email to:

   **nulldebuger@gmail.com**

   From the same email address that you used to register in HarfLink, with the subject line:

   > HarfLink – Account Deletion Request

   Please include:
   - Your HarfLink username (if available)
   - The email address used in the app
   - A short confirmation that you want your account to be permanently deleted

2. **From inside the app (if available)**

   If the app includes a "Contact support" or "Report a problem" option:
   - Open HarfLink  
   - Go to the support/contact section  
   - Send a message stating that you want to permanently delete your account

---

## What will be deleted

When we delete your account, we will remove:

- Your HarfLink profile and account identifiers  
- Game progress and match history linked to your account  
- In-app messages and social interactions linked to your account, where technically possible  
- Any other personal information that directly identifies you in our production databases  

Backup copies will be deleted automatically during regular backup rotation (normally within **90 days**).

---

## What we may still keep

We may retain some limited information, where required:

- Records needed to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations  
- Security and anti-fraud logs (for example, records of abusive behavior or cheating)  
- Aggregated or anonymized analytics data that can no longer be linked to you as an individual user  

This remaining data cannot be used to personally identify you.

---

## How long does it take?

We aim to process account deletion requests within **7 days**, and in most cases much faster.

If you have any questions about your data or this process, you can contact us at:

**nulldebuger@gmail.com**
